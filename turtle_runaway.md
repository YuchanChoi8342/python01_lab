# Turtle Runaway

## 1. Game Description

This is a Turtle Runaway game.

The player controls the green turtle using the arrow keys.
The orange triangle is the fish that runs away from the turtle.

## 2. Timer

The game has a 30-second timer.

The remaining time is displayed at the top of the game screen.
When the time reaches 0, the game ends.

## 3. Intelligent Turtle

The fish does not only move randomly.

When the turtle gets close to the fish, the fish changes its direction and tries to move away from the turtle.

When the turtle is far away, the fish moves randomly.

## 4. Scoring System

When the turtle catches the fish, the score increases by 1.

The fish and turtle are moved back to their starting positions after being caught.

The game ends when the turtle catches the fish 5 times.

## 5. Game Environment

The game has an underwater background with seaweed and a gray rock.

The green turtle is the chaser and the orange triangle is the runner.

## 6. Controls

* **Up Arrow**: Move forward
* **Down Arrow**: Move backward
* **Left Arrow**: Turn left
* **Right Arrow**: Turn right

## 7. Game Over

The game ends when either:

* The timer reaches 0.
* The turtle catches the fish 5 times.
